HRM 311 Demand & Staffing Forecasting

A production-style platform predicting Halifax 311 service-call volume at 30-minute granularity, framed around a real operational trade-off: under-staffing drives abandoned calls, over-staffing drives cost. NLP · Deep Learning

Quebec-French Acceptability Gap

Pretrained French language models learn Standard (France) French, yet roughly 7 million Canadians speak Quebec French, a variety with distinct syntax and anglicisms. Quantified that gap by scoring CamemBERT zero-shot via pseudo-log-likelihood, then fine-tuned it on QFrCoLA. Anglicisms stayed the hardest category even after fine-tuning. Compressed the fine-tuned model to ONNX with int8 quantization (442MB down to 111MB) and shipped it as a fully client-side demo running in-browser via Transformers.js, with no backend needed.

Data Engineering · Analytics

Canadian Mortgage Renewal Shock

A SQL data warehouse and dashboard modeling a live risk facing Canadian banks: mortgages from the 2020–2022 ultra-low-rate period renewing today at much higher rates. Pulls live Bank of Canada and StatCan data, no simulated numbers, into a SQLite star schema, computes payment shock and a provincial risk ranking, and forecasts rates with ARIMA(1,1,1). The posted 5-year rate now sits at 6.09%, up from 4.79%, driving an estimated 13.2% payment shock at renewal, with the highest exposure in Manitoba and Quebec. Presented through a Streamlit dashboard, a Power BI export, and an auto-generated PPTX stakeholder deck.
